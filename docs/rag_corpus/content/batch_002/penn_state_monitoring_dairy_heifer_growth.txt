English summary

Growth monitoring shows whether a heifer program is producing animals that are appropriately grown for the herd's calving-age and mature-size goals. Visual weight estimates are unreliable, so objective and repeatable measurements should be recorded over time. Useful measures include scale weight, heart girth with a suitable weight tape, height at the withers, hip height or width, and body length. Indirect methods are less accurate than a scale and must be used consistently on a hard, level surface with correct animal positioning.

Measurements should be plotted by age and compared with breed references or, preferably, herd-specific targets based on mature size and planned age at first calving. Growth charts help detect groups that are undersized, underweight, or overweight. Both chronic underfeeding and excessive energy intake can harm later performance, so the objective is steady, appropriate growth rather than maximum size.

中文摘要

生长监测用于判断后备母牛培育方案能否使牛只达到与本场初次产犊日龄和成年体型目标相匹配的生长水平。肉眼估重不可靠，应持续记录客观、可重复的测量。可用指标包括地磅体重、合适体重带测得的胸围、鬐甲高、髋高或髋宽，以及体长。间接估重不如地磅准确，测量时应保持方法一致，让牛站在坚硬平整地面并保持正确姿势。

测量值应按日龄绘图，并与品种参考或基于本场成年体型和初次产犊目标建立的本场曲线比较。生长曲线能发现整组牛偏小、偏轻或过肥的问题。长期营养不足和能量过量都可能损害后续表现，目标应是稳定、适宜的生长，而不是追求最大体型。

Scope / 适用范围

Reference charts are screening and management tools, not diagnostic thresholds. Breed, herd genetics, measurement method, diet, housing, and health all affect interpretation.

参考曲线是筛查和管理工具，不是诊断阈值；品种、本场遗传基础、测量方法、日粮、圈舍和健康状态都会影响解释。
